# Lesson 8 — Put It in the World

**Complete something real and let someone use it.**

## The workflow it is taught through

Ship on ground you own: the presentation lives on a public page anyone can visit, owned outright, no platform between the work and the world. Ideas need more than potential.

## Three ways in

- **Explore** — Show a finished thing to one person; write what they said.
- **Create** — Publish one thing with a real URL.
- **Build** — Make it self-contained so no vanished service can break it.
