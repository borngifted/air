# Lesson 7 — Build Your Way

**Turn what worked into your own repeatable workflow.**

## The workflow it is taught through

The film pipeline became a written, reusable skill the same day: generate, extract frames, chain, publish. If you ran it twice, it is real.

## Three ways in

- **Explore** — List your steps from the last thing you finished.
- **Create** — Write them so somebody else could run them.
- **Build** — Make the repeat one command.
