# Lesson 5 — Make It Clear

**Let hierarchy, contrast and space do the talking.**

## The workflow it is taught through

The logo-led system: the course site derives every color, angle and motion from its own mark, and every color pairing was contrast-checked before shipping. Design is how the message survives.

## Three ways in

- **Explore** — Find the one sentence that matters most; make it biggest.
- **Create** — One page, four sizes of type, nothing centered by accident.
- **Build** — Tokens: name your colors and spacing, use only the names.
