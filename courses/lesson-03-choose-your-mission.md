# Lesson 3 — Choose Your Mission

**Name the person, the problem, the message, the outcome.**

## The workflow it is taught through

The human-before-tool brief: who is this for, what should they know / feel / do, which decisions require human judgment — and is AI even appropriate here at all.

## Three ways in

- **Explore** — Describe one real person your idea helps.
- **Create** — Write the four-line brief for a real project.
- **Build** — Add the failure plan: what happens when it breaks?
