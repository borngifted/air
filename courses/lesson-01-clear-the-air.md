# Lesson 1 — Clear the Air

**Move from fear, hype and overload to one meaningful goal.**

## The workflow it is taught through

The mission sentence: *"I want to make ___ so that ___ can ___."* Cross out every goal that is somebody else's, every tool name that is not an outcome. Keep only what is yours. This sentence is the ticket into every other lesson.

## Three ways in

- **Explore** — Say your sentence out loud to somebody.
- **Create** — Write it, then cut it in half.
- **Build** — Write three, rank by who is actually waiting for it.

## Deeper: the Shift to Yet (Carol Dweck)

A fixed mindset says *"I'm not a tech person."* A growth mindset adds one word — *yet*. Errors and awkward prompts aren't proof you can't; they're your brain building the connection. In the presentation's practice suite, **The Shift to Yet** lets you flip fixed beliefs into growth ones and watch the neural links light up.
