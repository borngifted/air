# Lesson 6 — Challenge the Result

**Critique, verify, question, improve — judgment is the work.**

## The workflow it is taught through

The seams exercise: an early cut of the course film drifted — the person changed between clips, the poster text was gibberish. It was kept, visible, on purpose. Review every AI result for accuracy, clarity, representation, bias, privacy, ownership, accessibility and audience fit. Ask what the AI guessed and what a human changed.

## Three ways in

- **Explore** — Find three things wrong with an AI image you like.
- **Create** — Accuracy, representation, readability: check all three, fix one.
- **Build** — Break your own work deliberately and watch what it tells the user.
