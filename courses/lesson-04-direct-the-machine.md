# Lesson 4 — Direct the Machine

**Speak like a creative director — not a magic-prompt hunter.**

## The workflow it is taught through

Art direction blocks: subject, light, lens, palette, mood, and a strict negative list (no robots, no neon). When the tool tries to swap in a preset — someone else's look — decline it and keep your direction.

## Three ways in

- **Explore** — Direct one image with five deliberate words.
- **Create** — Write a reusable direction block for your project's look.
- **Build** — Chain outputs: feed one result in as the next input.
