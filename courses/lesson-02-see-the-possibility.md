# Lesson 2 — See the Possibility

**Understand what makes finished work useful — not impressive, useful.**

## The workflow it is taught through

The chained film: eight brand stills morphed into one continuous motion piece by matching each clip's first frame to the last frame before it. The film behind the presentation was made exactly this way — useful because a real audience can watch it, not because the tool was clever.

## Three ways in

- **Explore** — Watch one finished AI piece and name who it helps.
- **Create** — Collect three finished works and write what made each land.
- **Build** — Take one apart: list every human decision inside it.
