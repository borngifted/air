# AiR Commercial Shoot Book

## Four human moves · Four 30-second films

**Version 1.0 · September 2026**  
Prepared for **AiR — AI Readiness** and **Digi2U.org**  
Prepared by **Manus AI**

This shoot book turns the AiR method into four connected commercials: **Clear, Direct, Judge, and Make**. Each film is designed as a 30-second hero edit with a 15-second cutdown and 6-second bumper. Together they form a single story about an intergenerational team learning to use AI with purpose and judgment.[1] [2]

> **Campaign line: Stop learning AI. Learn to move with it.**

## 1. Campaign overview

| Film | Title | Tension | Human move | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01 · Clear** | **One Person. One Change.** | Too many tools create pressure and paralysis | Choose one real person and one useful change | A clear mission card replaces a wall of tool names |
| **02 · Direct** | **Better for Who?** | A vague request makes the system guess | Give audience, task, limits, and finish line | A useful draft replaces four random outputs |
| **03 · Judge** | **Sure Is Not a Source.** | Fluent language makes a wrong detail feel true | Mark claims, check evidence, and match review to stakes | A wrong date is caught before public release |
| **04 · Make** | **Let the Work Meet Someone.** | Perfection and planning keep the work hidden | Finish a small version, show a person, and revise | Real feedback makes the artifact useful |

All four spots take place in the same community creative room over one day. The continuity makes the campaign feel like one movement rather than four unrelated lessons. The team begins with noise, learns to direct, catches a mistake, and releases something useful.

## 2. Production rules

### 2.1 Format package

| Master | Frame | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Hero commercial | 30 seconds | 16:9 master, 4:5 adaptation, 9:16 adaptation |
| Social cutdown | 15 seconds | 4:5 and 9:16 |
| Bumper | 6 seconds | 16:9 and 9:16 |
| Silent event loop | 20–30 seconds | 16:9 with on-screen copy carrying the entire idea |

Shoot open-gate or at least UHD 4K so each composition can be reframed vertically. Keep the essential face, hand action, and text prop inside the center 60% of frame. Capture ten seconds of clean room tone and a clean plate for every setup.

### 2.2 Shared cast

| Character | Casting direction | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Jalen** | African American man, 28–42, warm creative-leader energy; confident enough to admit when he does not know | Adult learner and community creator |
| **Nia** | Black girl, playing age 9–11; observant, direct, never precocious for comedy | Young learner who asks the clearest questions |
| **Ms. Alvarez** | Afro-Latina or brown woman, 35–55; educator or community-program lead energy | Facilitator who turns ideas into visible steps |
| **Ms. Rosa** | Black or brown woman, 50–70; natural presence, practical and specific | The real person whose feedback closes the loop |
| **Community ensemble** | Four to eight Black and brown children ages 8+, caregivers, creators, and older adults | Shows AiR as a mixed-age practice community |

The child performer must have a guardian present, age-appropriate hours, private changing space, and no personally identifying information in props or screens. Do not show a child independently publishing to a public AI account.

### 2.3 Shared location

Use one adaptable community room, library lab, nonprofit studio, classroom, or creative office with:

| Zone | Visual purpose |
|---|---|
| **Mission wall** | Sticky notes, tool clutter, then one clean mission card |
| **Direction table** | Laptop, printed brief cards, markers, and a shared screen |
| **Check station** | Printed source, highlighters, claim ledger, and phone |
| **Make table** | Paper prototype, printer, tape, cutting mat, camera, and final artifact |
| **Community wall** | Finished work, feedback cards, AiR move numbers, and final group frame |

### 2.4 Art direction

Deep Air, Paper, black, wood, denim, and natural skin tones carry the room. Go appears in tape, marker, or one clothing accent. Spark appears once per film, usually when the human move becomes clear. Avoid visible third-party AI logos. Build a generic screen interface in post so outputs, prompts, and sources remain legally clean and readable.

### 2.5 Sound and captions

Use a restrained four-beat percussion identity across all films. Layer real sounds—marker, paper, keyboard, mouse click, tape pull, printer, and room breath—so the work feels physical. Every spoken line receives burned-in or platform captions. The story must still work with sound off.

## 3. Commercial 01 — Clear

# One Person. One Change.

![Visual reference: young learner and educator making together](https://borngifted.github.io/air/media/air-campaign-move.png)

### Creative intent

The film begins with AI pressure as physical clutter. The solution is not a better app. It is one question that a child and adult can both understand: **Who are we helping?**

| Production element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 30 seconds |
| **Hero talent** | Nia and Jalen; Ms. Rosa appears in the proof beat |
| **Location** | Mission wall and worktable |
| **Props** | Phone, 20 tool-name sticky notes, marker, Paper mission card, official pantry flyer, laptop |
| **Wardrobe** | Jalen in Deep Air overshirt and cream tee; Nia in denim with one Spark hair tie or pin |
| **Light** | Morning side light; clutter cooler and flatter, mission moment warmer and cleaner |
| **Camera** | Restless macro inserts at first; stable 35 mm two-shot once the mission is chosen |

### 30-second visual beats and script

| Time | Picture | Dialogue / voiceover | On-screen copy | Camera, edit, and sound |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:02** | Phone scroll: “10 AI tools you need today.” Another list. Another notification. Jalen’s thumb stops. | **VO — Nia:** “Everybody kept telling us which AI tool we needed.” | `MORE TOOLS.` | Three fast macro cuts. Notification taps build an uneven rhythm. |
| **0:02–0:05** | Overhead: the table and wall fill with sticky notes carrying generic tool categories. Jalen adds one more, then exhales. | **VO — Nia:** “The list kept getting bigger.” | `MORE NOISE.` | Slight handheld drift. Paper and tape sounds crowd the mix. |
| **0:05–0:08** | Nia steps into frame, looks at the wall, then at Jalen. | **Nia:** “Who are we helping?” | The copy disappears. | Cut to still, centered two-shot. The noisy sound bed drops out. |
| **0:08–0:12** | Jalen looks toward Ms. Rosa organizing pantry flyers across the room. Nia pulls down the first tool note. | **Jalen:** “Ms. Rosa needs tomorrow’s pantry hours before work.” | `ONE PERSON.` | Slow push. First clean bass hit. |
| **0:12–0:16** | Together they cross out the remaining tool notes. Nia writes a mission card: “Help Ms. Rosa find tomorrow’s pantry hours in under one minute.” | **VO — Nia:** “So we crossed out the tools and chose one change.” | `ONE CHANGE.` | Marker scratch becomes beat two. Spark tape frames the mission. |
| **0:16–0:21** | Jalen uses the mission to draft a short mobile message. Nia points to the date on the official flyer. | **Jalen:** “AI helped us draft.” **Nia:** “Then we checked the real flyer.” | `DRAFT.` then `CHECK.` | Match cut from mission card to screen. Beat three lands on the check mark. |
| **0:21–0:25** | Ms. Rosa reads the finished message on the phone. Her thumb finds the hours immediately. She nods. | **Ms. Rosa:** “That is what I needed.” | `USEFUL.` | Warm close-up. Hold long enough to see the result, not a staged reaction. |
| **0:25–0:27** | Nia and Jalen place the single mission card in the center of the now-clear wall. | **VO — Jalen:** “Purpose before tools.” | `01 · CLEAR` | Fourth beat. Lock-off. |
| **0:27–0:30** | Deep Air end card. AiR mark reveals; the **i** dot lands last. | **VO — Jalen:** “Stop learning AI. Learn to move with it.” | `JOIN AIR FREE`  
`AGES 8 TO ADULT`  
`borngifted.github.io/air/` | Hold logo and URL for three seconds. |

### Performance notes

Nia is not delivering a clever punch line. She asks the obvious human question everyone else forgot. Jalen’s realization should feel relieved, not embarrassed. Ms. Rosa gives a small practical nod; do not play the moment as a testimonial.

### 15-second cutdown

| Time | Edit |
|---:|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Tool lists and sticky-note clutter. **VO:** “Everybody told us which AI tool we needed.” |
| 0:03–0:06 | Nia: “Who are we helping?” Sound drops. |
| 0:06–0:10 | Mission card written and draft checked against flyer. **VO:** “One person. One change.” |
| 0:10–0:12 | Ms. Rosa finds the hours. `USEFUL.` |
| 0:12–0:15 | `01 · CLEAR` · `PURPOSE BEFORE TOOLS` · AiR · `JOIN FREE` |

### 6-second bumper

Tool clutter. Nia clears the wall and holds one mission card.

> **NIA:** “Too many tools? Choose one mission.”  
> **END CARD:** `AIR · MOVE WITH IT. · FREE`

### Must-have coverage

Capture macro inserts of the thumb scroll, tool notes, marker crossing-out, mission card, official flyer, phone result, and Spark tape. Record a clean vertical two-shot of Nia asking the question and a separate three-second end-card plate.

## 4. Commercial 02 — Direct

# Better for Who?

### Creative intent

This film makes prompting physical and understandable. A vague instruction creates four random guesses. A simple director card gives the team a shared picture of what good means.

| Production element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 30 seconds |
| **Hero talent** | Jalen, Nia, and Ms. Alvarez |
| **Location** | Direction table with shared monitor |
| **Props** | Laptop, four deliberately different flyer drafts, director card, black and Go markers, event details |
| **Wardrobe** | Ms. Alvarez in cream with Deep Air jacket; Jalen continues; Nia retains Spark accent |
| **Light** | Clean midday daylight; practical desk light motivates the screen |
| **Camera** | 50 mm for dialogue; overhead for the brief; fast graphic match cuts between bad drafts |

### 30-second visual beats and script

| Time | Picture | Dialogue / voiceover | On-screen copy | Camera, edit, and sound |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:03** | Jalen types: “Make this better.” Four wildly different flyer drafts flash—formal, childish, crowded, and vague. | **Jalen:** “I asked AI to make it better.” | `MAKE IT BETTER.` | Each wrong draft lands on a clipped percussion hit. |
| **0:03–0:05** | Jalen looks at the screen. | **Jalen:** “It guessed.” | `IT GUESSED.` | Hard stop. Hold on his honest reaction. |
| **0:05–0:08** | Ms. Alvarez pulls a blank director card into frame. | **Ms. Alvarez:** “Better for who?” | `FOR WHO?` | Top-down snap to the card. Pencil tap starts the four-beat rhythm. |
| **0:08–0:14** | Nia and Jalen fill four boxes: PERSON, JOB, LIMITS, CHECK. | **Nia:** “Parents coming tonight.” **Jalen:** “One warm sentence. Keep the time and address.” | `PERSON.` `JOB.` `LIMITS.` `CHECK.` | One word per beat. Insert hands, not floating graphics. |
| **0:14–0:18** | Jalen adds: “Ask if anything is missing.” He submits the clear brief. | **Ms. Alvarez:** “Now give it a finish line.” | `WHAT DOES DONE LOOK LIKE?` | Slow push toward the final box. |
| **0:18–0:23** | A clean, readable message appears. Nia checks the time and address against the event card. | **Nia:** “Now the job is clear.” **Ms. Alvarez:** “Because you gave it direction.” | `CLEAR IN.` `USEFUL OUT.` | Hold on the human check so the useful result is credited to clear direction and review—not machine understanding. |
| **0:23–0:26** | Jalen circles the final draft and pins the director card beside it. | **Ms. Alvarez:** “You choose what good means.” | `02 · DIRECT` | Fourth beat. Camera becomes perfectly still. |
| **0:26–0:30** | Paper end card with Deep Air mark and one Go rule. | **VO — Jalen:** “Clarity before cleverness. Move with AiR.” | `STOP LEARNING AI.`  
`LEARN TO MOVE WITH IT.`  
`JOIN FREE` | Hold URL and logo. |

### Performance notes

Ms. Alvarez is a facilitator, not an expert who rescues the team. She asks the next useful question. Jalen owns the direction. Nia validates that the draft fits a real audience.

### 15-second cutdown

| Time | Edit |
|---:|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | “Make this better.” Four random drafts. **Jalen:** “It guessed.” |
| 0:03–0:05 | **Ms. Alvarez:** “Better for who?” |
| 0:05–0:10 | PERSON · JOB · LIMITS · CHECK fill in on the director card. |
| 0:10–0:12 | Clean draft; Nia checks the details. **Ms. Alvarez:** “You choose what good means.” |
| 0:12–0:15 | `02 · DIRECT` · `CLARITY BEFORE CLEVERNESS` · AiR · `JOIN FREE` |

### 6-second bumper

Four random drafts flash. The director card slaps down.

> **MS. ALVAREZ:** “Vague in. Guess out. Give direction.”  
> **END CARD:** `AIR · MOVE WITH IT.`

### Must-have coverage

Capture each bad draft as a clean screen plate, the full director card, each hand-written box, the final checked message, and neutral reaction close-ups. Record the line “You choose what good means” in a clean close-up and a clean voiceover take.

## 5. Commercial 03 — Judge

# Sure Is Not a Source.

![Visual reference: creator checking AI work](https://borngifted.github.io/air/media/air-campaign-judge.png)

### Creative intent

The film turns verification into a visible reflex. A confident answer contains one wrong date. Nia’s question stops the team before they share it. The tension is real but low enough for an eight-year-old to understand.

| Production element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 30 seconds |
| **Hero talent** | Nia and Jalen; Ms. Alvarez joins the stakes beat |
| **Location** | Check station beside community wall |
| **Props** | Draft event message, phone, official web page printout, highlighters, three-column claim ledger |
| **Wardrobe** | Continue campaign wardrobe; add Coral highlighter only for the incorrect claim |
| **Light** | Slightly harder side light for focus; warmer after correction |
| **Camera** | Controlled close-ups; one sharp rack focus from confident sentence to missing source |

### 30-second visual beats and script

| Time | Picture | Dialogue / voiceover | On-screen copy | Camera, edit, and sound |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:03** | A polished message on screen: “The community lab meets Saturday at 2 PM.” Jalen’s cursor hovers over POST. | **Jalen:** “Sounds good.” | `SATURDAY · 2 PM` | Smooth, confident music phrase. Cursor click begins but does not land. |
| **0:03–0:06** | Nia puts one finger between the cursor and trackpad. | **Nia:** “Where did Saturday come from?” | `WHERE DID THAT COME FROM?` | Music cuts. Tight close-up on her calm face. |
| **0:06–0:09** | Jalen looks back at the answer. | **Jalen:** “It sounds sure.” **Nia:** “Sure is not a source.” | `CONFIDENCE ≠ EVIDENCE` | Use the not-equal sign only with the spoken plain-language line. |
| **0:09–0:15** | They print or open the message and mark DATE, TIME, and QUOTE. Three claims move into the ledger: CLAIM · SOURCE · ACTION. | **VO — Ms. Alvarez:** “Mark the claims. Find the source. Decide what changes.” | `CLAIM.` `SOURCE.` `ACTION.` | Highlighter strokes become three beats. |
| **0:15–0:20** | Official event page shows **Friday at 2 PM**. Nia circles it. Jalen changes the draft. | **Nia:** “Official page says Friday.” **Jalen:** “Good catch.” | `FRIDAY · VERIFIED` | Coral strike-through becomes Go check. |
| **0:20–0:24** | Ms. Alvarez points to three cards: joke, event time, health advice. The pause grows with the consequence. | **Ms. Alvarez:** “More consequence, more pause.” | `MORE CONSEQUENCE.` `MORE PAUSE.` | Widen to include the group and stakes cards. |
| **0:24–0:27** | Jalen posts the corrected message. Nia checks the official source one last time. | **VO — Nia:** “Fast answer. Human check.” | `03 · JUDGE` | Fourth beat lands on the verified post. |
| **0:27–0:30** | Deep Air end card. Paper mark. Spark underline on CHECK. | **VO — Jalen:** “Check before you trust.” | `CHECK BEFORE YOU TRUST.`  
`JOIN AIR FREE`  
`borngifted.github.io/air/` | Hold for three seconds. |

### Performance notes

Nia stops the action without panic. Jalen receives the correction without defensiveness. The film models a healthy review culture: finding a problem is success. The wrong date must be clearly fictional and must not resemble a real public event.

### 15-second cutdown

| Time | Edit |
|---:|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Polished wrong date. Cursor hovers over POST. **Jalen:** “Sounds good.” |
| 0:03–0:06 | Nia blocks the click. “Where did Saturday come from?” |
| 0:06–0:10 | Claims highlighted; official page says Friday. “Sure is not a source.” |
| 0:10–0:12 | Corrected message posts. `VERIFIED.` |
| 0:12–0:15 | `03 · JUDGE` · `CHECK BEFORE YOU TRUST` · AiR · `JOIN FREE` |

### 6-second bumper

Cursor over a confident claim. Nia stops it. Official source slides into frame.

> **NIA:** “Sounds sure? Check the source.”  
> **END CARD:** `AIR · HUMAN CHECK.`

### Must-have coverage

Capture the wrong claim, hovering cursor, Nia’s hand stop, all three highlighted claim types, official source, corrected draft, claim ledger, stakes cards, and verified post. Record clean insert plates with no personal names, real locations, or account information.

## 6. Commercial 04 — Make

# Let the Work Meet Someone.

![Visual reference: intergenerational AiR community](https://borngifted.github.io/air/media/air-campaign-community.png)

### Creative intent

The campaign ends by moving work from the table into another person’s hands. The first version is imperfect. That is the point. Real feedback reveals what planning could not.

| Production element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 30 seconds |
| **Hero talent** | Jalen, Nia, Ms. Rosa, and full community ensemble |
| **Location** | Make table, community wall, and doorway or corridor for release |
| **Props** | Folded paper prototype, phone mockup, printer, tape, large-type revision, feedback card, community board |
| **Wardrobe** | Continue wardrobe; ensemble adds cream, denim, brown, and one Go accent each |
| **Light** | Late-afternoon warmth; practical lamps and window edge light create completion without sentimentality |
| **Camera** | Tactile macro work, then wider human frames as the artifact enters the room |

### 30-second visual beats and script

| Time | Picture | Dialogue / voiceover | On-screen copy | Camera, edit, and sound |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:03** | Jalen makes tiny adjustments to a flyer on screen. Versions 7, 8, and 9 sit hidden in a folder. | **Jalen:** “I kept waiting for version one to be perfect.” | `STILL PLANNING.` | Clock tick and mouse clicks. Tight frame feels boxed in. |
| **0:03–0:06** | Nia folds the paper draft once and slides it toward Ms. Rosa. | **Nia:** “Then it never meets anyone.” | `SHOW ONE PERSON.` | Paper slide becomes beat one. Camera widens. |
| **0:06–0:11** | Ms. Rosa looks at the flyer, then at the phone version. She pinches to zoom. | **Ms. Rosa:** “I can find the time. The address is too small.” | `REAL USE.` | Hold on the actual friction. No exaggerated reaction. |
| **0:11–0:16** | Jalen increases the address, Nia shortens a sentence, Ms. Alvarez checks contrast. | **VO — Jalen:** “A real person showed us what planning could not.” | `LISTEN.` `CHANGE.` | Three work inserts on three beats. |
| **0:16–0:20** | New version prints. Ms. Rosa reads it without zooming and points to the address. | **Ms. Rosa:** “Now I can use it.” | `USEFUL BEATS PERFECT.` | Warm close-up. Printer becomes beat four. |
| **0:20–0:24** | The team documents a five-step process card: Mission, Brief, Check, Revise, Share. | **Ms. Alvarez:** “Keep the steps. Run it again.” | `MAKE YOUR WAY REPEATABLE.` | Overhead lock-off; hands finish the workflow. |
| **0:24–0:27** | The finished flyer goes on the community wall. A child, parent, and older adult scan or read it together. | **VO — Nia:** “Make it. Show it. Change it.” | `04 · MAKE` | Camera moves from artifact to people using it. |
| **0:27–0:30** | Paper end card with Deep Air mark. Go CTA. | **VO — Jalen:** “Put it in the world.” | `STOP LEARNING AI.`  
`LEARN TO MOVE WITH IT.`  
`JOIN AIR FREE` | End on one confident four-beat resolve. |

### Performance notes

The feedback is specific and kind. Ms. Rosa is a co-maker, not a grateful recipient. Jalen changes the work immediately. The ensemble uses the final artifact naturally while continuing their own activity.

### 15-second cutdown

| Time | Edit |
|---:|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Hidden versions and tiny edits. **Jalen:** “I kept waiting for perfect.” |
| 0:03–0:07 | Nia slides the first version to Ms. Rosa. “Then it never meets anyone.” |
| 0:07–0:10 | Ms. Rosa: “The address is too small.” Team revises. |
| 0:10–0:12 | Final version in use. `USEFUL BEATS PERFECT.` |
| 0:12–0:15 | `04 · MAKE` · `PUT IT IN THE WORLD` · AiR · `JOIN FREE` |

### 6-second bumper

Hidden draft. Paper slides to a real person. Quick feedback. Bigger type prints.

> **VO — JALEN:** “Still planning? Make version one.”  
> **END CARD:** `AIR · PUT IT IN THE WORLD.`

### Must-have coverage

Capture version folders, small type, paper slide, pinch-to-zoom, Ms. Rosa’s point, live revision, contrast check, printer output, five-step workflow, final wall placement, and intergenerational use. Capture a clean wide frame of the full ensemble for the campaign supercut.

## 11. Post-production map

| Pass | Work |
|---|---|
| **Assembly** | Protect performance and make the human decision readable before adding graphic pace |
| **Graphics** | Use Barlow Condensed, 01–04 numerals, Deep Air/Paper, one Spark reveal, and safe margins |
| **Screens** | Replace with generic AiR-style UI; ensure every claim and date is fictional |
| **Sound** | Build one four-beat identity, then give each film a distinctive physical sound |
| **Color** | Warm skin, deep green shadows, clean Paper highlights; avoid cyan “tech” grade |
| **Access** | Burned-in review caption file, platform caption file, transcript, audio-description notes, no-flash check |

## 12. Campaign supercut

A 45- or 60-second launch film can combine the question from each spot:

> **NIA:** Who are we helping?  
> **MS. ALVAREZ:** Better for who?  
> **NIA:** Where did that come from?  
> **MS. ROSA:** Now I can use it.

Cut the four human moves on the shared four-beat sound identity. End on the ensemble placing all four cards—CLEAR, DIRECT, JUDGE, MAKE—around the AiR mark.

| Check | Confirm before wrap |
