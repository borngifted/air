↓ Here's the whole thing in plain words

What is this, in plain words?

AI tools are everywhere, and most advice is noise. Learn twelve apps. Master the magic prompts. Keep up or fall behind. That noise makes people feel late to something they never signed up for.

AIR flips it. You don't start with a tool — you start with something you actually want to make, for a real person. Then you use AI the way a director uses a camera crew: give clear directions, look hard at what comes back, keep what's good, fix what's wrong, and finish.

The goal of everything here is one sentence: clear the air, un-clutter the mindset, communicate with intention.

What will I actually do?

Eight short lessons, each one ends with something finished — a page someone can read, a plan, a piece you can share. Move a dot, or your hand on camera, to travel through them.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Every lesson has three ways in: Explore (guided, great with kids), Create (your own project), Build (for people who want to go under the hood). Pick per lesson, switch anytime.

What does it cost? What do you collect?

Nothing and nothing. No account, no email, no tracking. Your practice answers save only on your own device.

Who made this?

Digi2U.org — a community media-and-technology training organization. AIR is their free AI readiness program, taught through real projects with the seams left visible.
