Pick your level. Change it whenever.

Not difficulty tiers, and never decided by age. An eight-year-old might Build. A developer might Explore. Every path ends in a finished thing.

Explore

Create

Most people, most of the time. Real decisions, real tradeoffs, your own subject matter.

Build

Under the hood. Workflow, automation, code, production systems that keep working after you leave.

Generated with Seedance 2.5 using the start-frame / end-frame chaining method from the Affirm' project: each clip begins on the exact frame the previous one ended. Clutter → stillness → making → the world.

The chain, exposed

Each clip was generated starting from the exact final frame of the one before it, so the person, light and world carry through. Below is the first attempt in pieces — four independent clips joined by interpolated transitions. Kept on purpose: comparing the two versions is the lesson.

16:9 version ↓ First attempt, seams and all ↓

Tap any piece to play it. This is Challenge the Result in practice: look closely and you'll find the seams — identity drifting between clips, morphing in the transitions, gibberish text on the poster. Seeing them is the skill AIR teaches.
